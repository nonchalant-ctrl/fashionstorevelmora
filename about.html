OUR STORY

Born from a Vision

VELMORA was created with a simple idea, to bring timeless, high quality, fashion peices to people who moves differently. We saw a need for a brand that brings elegance, comfort and everyday style, and thats exactly what we set out to build. What started as a passion for fashion has grown into a brand dedicated to helping you express your unique style, with pieces that fits every moments of your journey
